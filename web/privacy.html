Privacy Policy

KashU · Last updated 21 October 2026

KashU does not have user accounts, does not have a server that stores your information, and does not collect, sell or share personal data. Your portfolio stays on your own device.

What KashU stores, and where

  • On the mobile app: everything you enter — holdings, transactions, settings — is saved only on your device, in a database encrypted with AES-256. The encryption key is held in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore.
  • On the web app: the same data is stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB), encrypted with a key kept in that same browser. Clearing your browser's site data permanently erases it.
  • None of this is ever uploaded. There is no copy of your portfolio anywhere but your device.
  • On Android, KashU's data is also kept out of Google's automatic backups and phone-to-phone transfers: it is encrypted with a key that never leaves the original phone, so a copy could not be read anyway. To move your portfolio, use Settings › Export Data and import the file on the new device.

Network requests

KashU connects to the internet for one purpose: fetching public market prices. These are read-only requests to third-party services:

  • Yahoo Finance and Stooq — stock, ETF and metals prices
  • CoinGecko and CryptoCompare — cryptocurrency prices
  • MFAPI.in — Indian mutual fund NAVs
  • open.er-api.com — currency exchange rates

To be clear about what this means: when KashU refreshes a price, the symbol being looked up (for example RELIANCE.NS) and your device's IP address are visible to that provider, as with any web request. Those providers have their own privacy policies. KashU sends them nothing else — no identifier, no account, no portfolio quantities or values.

On the web version only, these requests pass through a proxy we operate at kash-u.vercel.app, because browsers block direct calls to those providers. The proxy forwards the request and the response, and can only reach that fixed list of price services. It never receives your portfolio.

Crash reporting

KashU can optionally report crashes to help fix bugs. It is off by default and only runs if you turn it on in Settings. Even when enabled, network request details are deliberately stripped before sending, so the symbols you track are not included.

What KashU does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No accounts, sign-in, email collection or newsletters
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected

Your data, your control

You can export everything as a JSON file at any time from Settings, import that file on another device, and deleting the app (or clearing site data on web) removes it permanently. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is no deletion request to make — and no way for us to recover your data if you lose it, so keep a backup.

Children

KashU is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
